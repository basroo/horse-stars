# Recommended visual assets

The website reuses the supplied logo, horse imagery, original rarity cards, Champion cards, and the real Collector’s Book cover extracted from the supplied PDF. No brand or card artwork has been recreated.

## Collector’s Book

**Recommended asset:** A professionally photographed or rendered product mockup of the physical book, slightly open, showing interior collection/checklist pages. Use the real artwork, including the eventual English cover if one is produced.

**Recommended size:** 2000 × 1600 px PNG or WebP; transparent or neutral cream background. Keep an uncropped version with room for shadows. Current showcase uses the real Czech cover and separate real cards; the artwork language is disclosed in its caption.

## Horse Stars News

**Recommended asset:** One commissioned featured episode and a matching thumbnail showing its actual content. Use a strong central subject and leave space at the top and lower left for the website’s news identity and title treatment.

**Recommended size:** 1920 × 1080 px WebP or JPG. Supply the official YouTube video ID and channel URL. Current image is a clearly named `horse-stars-news-placeholder.webp`, cropped from the existing supplied hero artwork. No fake episode or video is playable.

## Final brand moment

**Recommended asset:** A distinct wide cinematic horse scene so the conclusion has its own visual rather than reusing the hero. Three or more horses in motion, with calm darker space on the left for type; avoid embedded logos or text.

**Recommended size:** 2400 × 1200 px WebP or JPG, plus a 1080 × 1440 px mobile crop if available.
